Banks Are Moving AI From the Chatbot Window to the Money-Movement Layer

Banks and finance chiefs are deploying AI agents capable of orchestrating end-to-end workflows across compliance, treasury, and payments — a significant step beyond the customer-facing chatbots that defined the first wave. The shift means AI is now touching actual money movement, not just answering questions about it. RBC has committed to delivering $700M–$1B in net benefit through AI execution by end of 2027, and it's becoming a template others are watching closely. PYMNTS ↗

Fiserv Bets on Embedded Finance to Close the SMB Banking Gap

Fiserv is expanding its embedded finance offering for small business customers, targeting the growing gap between what traditional banks offer SMBs and what they actually need. The play centers on integrating lending, payments, and cash flow tools directly into accounting and commerce platforms. It's a logical extension for a company that already sits in the infrastructure layer of thousands of banks — the question is whether it can execute at the pace fintechs are setting. Finextra ↗

Q2 Holdings Lands Three New Digital Banking Contracts in One Quarter

Q2 Holdings closed three new digital banking platform deals in Q1, a sign that community banks and credit unions are still actively replacing aging online banking infrastructure despite macro uncertainty. The wins span two credit unions and a regional bank, all migrating from legacy systems. Q2's pitch — that a single platform can serve both retail and commercial customers — appears to be resonating at institutions that are tired of stitching together point solutions. American Banker ↗
